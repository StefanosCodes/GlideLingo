# Mechanic patterns

Use this library to select a mechanism after the target behavior and emotional transition are known. Mechanics are hypotheses, not mandatory features.

## Capability milestones

- **Emotional job:** turn effort into credible competence and identity.
- **Pattern:** name a real-world “I can…” ability; show the evidence that unlocked it; offer a small next use or review.
- **Measure:** delayed performance, next useful action, milestone comprehension.
- **Guardrail:** do not unlock from attendance alone when the copy implies mastery.

## Progress and proximity

- **Emotional job:** reduce uncertainty and make completion feel reachable.
- **Pattern:** show progress at the scale relevant to the current decision: activity, lesson, module, then course. Pair percentage with a meaningful destination.
- **Measure:** lesson starts and completions, abandonment by step, learner understanding of what progress means.
- **Guardrail:** do not create artificial steps, moving goals, or inflated percentages.

## Consistency and streaks

- **Emotional job:** help a learner recognize a habit and return with less deliberation.
- **Pattern:** let the learner choose a cadence; count meaningful practice; consider weekly consistency alongside consecutive days; preserve completed learning after a lapse; provide transparent repair or restart language.
- **Measure:** meaningful practice days, return after lapse, learning minutes, delayed learning outcomes.
- **Guardrail:** track the learner's goal rather than loyalty to the app. Never sell relief from an arbitrary break rule.

## Missions and short goals

- **Emotional job:** convert a distant aspiration into a clear, finishable episode.
- **Pattern:** one real-world mission, a visible end, bounded challenge, and closure that names what changed.
- **Measure:** start-to-finish rate, retries, demonstrated outcome, voluntary next mission.
- **Guardrail:** do not add chores that optimize completion counts while displacing better practice.

## Informational feedback

- **Emotional job:** transform uncertainty into competence and recoverable effort.
- **Pattern:** respond quickly; identify the meaningful contrast; preserve the learner's input; provide a next attempt or example.
- **Measure:** correction on retry, later transfer, help use, repeated error rate.
- **Guardrail:** avoid generic praise, unexplained correctness, or punishment that makes experimentation feel unsafe.

## Celebrations

- **Emotional job:** mark closure, make earned progress memorable, and strengthen learner identity.
- **Pattern:** connect copy and expression to the exact effort or ability; vary intensity by rarity; let the learner continue immediately.
- **Measure:** comprehension of the achievement, next action, qualitative sentiment, reduced-motion parity.
- **Guardrail:** do not celebrate routine taps like major victories or interrupt every correct answer.

## Choice and personalization

- **Emotional job:** support autonomy and personal relevance.
- **Pattern:** offer a small number of consequential choices such as goal, context, challenge, or session length; explain effects; preserve easy revision.
- **Measure:** choice completion, later changes, follow-through, learning outcome by selected path.
- **Guardrail:** avoid cosmetic choice that disguises a fixed path or defaults that pressure the learner.

## Return cues and notifications

- **Emotional job:** reconnect a genuine intention to an appropriate moment.
- **Pattern:** use learner-selected timing or context, name a concrete small action, deep-link to it, cap repetitions, and stop when irrelevant.
- **Measure:** notification-to-meaningful-practice conversion, disable rate, dismissals, return without notification, learning outcome.
- **Guardrail:** no guilt, relationship simulation, false urgency, escalating harassment, or notification copy that hides the actual destination.

## Social and sharing

- **Emotional job:** create belonging, recognition, or cooperative accountability.
- **Pattern:** opt-in sharing of a real capability or artifact; cooperative goals before global rank; private by default.
- **Measure:** consented shares, helpful interactions, retention across participant types, report or mute rate.
- **Guardrail:** never fabricate activity, expose learning vulnerability by default, or equate rank with language ability.

## Rewards and currencies

- **Emotional job:** provide symbolic closure or enable meaningful learner choice.
- **Pattern:** prefer direct capability, access, customization, or collected artifacts over a layered economy.
- **Measure:** whether the reward changes useful behavior without reducing learning quality or autonomy.
- **Guardrail:** no confusing exchange rates, random paid outcomes, expiring value, or rewards that become the only reason to learn.
