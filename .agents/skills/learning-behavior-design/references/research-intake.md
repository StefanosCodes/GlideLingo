# Research intake and maintenance

Use this process when adding the user's research, analytics, interviews, screenshots, competitor observations, or academic references.

## Preserve the raw input

Do not silently rewrite a source into a universal rule. Record enough provenance to find it again:

- title or short identifier;
- author or origin;
- source type: primary study, review, product report, analytics, interview, observation, opinion, or hypothesis;
- publication or observation date;
- direct link or repository path;
- population, product context, and sample when known;
- relevant excerpt or faithful summary;
- user interpretation, kept separate from the source's claim.

If the source is an image or competitor screen, record the visible state, preceding action, platform, locale, date, and what is inferred rather than visible.

## Create a decision record

For each claim that may influence GlideLingo, add:

- **Claim:** the narrow supported statement.
- **Evidence strength:** strong, moderate, directional, anecdotal, or unknown, with a reason.
- **Applies to:** learner segment, journey moment, and mechanic.
- **Product implication:** the decision it supports or challenges.
- **Limitations:** transfer risk, confounds, missing population, short duration, self-report, or vendor interest.
- **Contradictions:** evidence or product observations pointing elsewhere.
- **Validation:** the smallest ethical test or observation needed in GlideLingo.

## Maintain the ledger

Add only decision-relevant syntheses to `research-ledger.md`; keep bulky raw material in an appropriately named project research folder when one exists. Prefer direct papers and first-party product disclosures over derivative summaries. Mark causal, correlational, qualitative, and speculative evidence accurately.

When new evidence conflicts with an existing entry, preserve both, narrow the claim, and explain what context may account for the difference. Do not resolve disagreement by counting sources.

Review any decision-critical evidence again before a consequential implementation or experiment. URLs, product behavior, and company claims can change.
