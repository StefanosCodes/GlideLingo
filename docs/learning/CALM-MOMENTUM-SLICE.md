# Calm-momentum learning evidence slice

## Decision

GlideLingo's first gameful loop makes a narrow learned capability emotionally visible. It records attempts, support, path completion, review need, and weekly consistency separately; it does not add XP, currency, public competition, or a punitive daily streak. A weekly rhythm streak counts consecutive weeks in which the learner meets a chosen two-, three-, or five-day practice target.

## Learner and moment

- **Learner:** a self-directed beginner completing the first authored Greek sound mission.
- **Entry:** Home, the lesson sitting, Profile, or contextual strengthening practice.
- **Before:** Greek script may feel unfamiliar, and the learner cannot tell whether finishing a lesson means anything stuck.
- **After:** the learner understands whether the capability was introduced, practiced through recovery, or demonstrated in a fresh pattern, plus what useful action comes next.

## Outcomes

- **Learning outcome:** recognize α, ε, and ι when decoding a new Greek syllable.
- **Target behavior:** complete the fresh checkpoint and return for a varied review when it becomes due.
- **Behavior outcome:** choose a sustainable weekly practice rhythm and distribute meaningful practice across it.
- **Non-goals:** retained capability, general reading proficiency, XP, badges, currencies, leaderboards, notifications, or cross-device durability.

## Mechanism

- A first-try fresh checkpoint can mark the narrow capability `Demonstrated`.
- Recovery after an incorrect checkpoint remains `Practiced`; it never becomes fake mastery.
- Replaying with more support never erases stronger prior evidence.
- Review uses a separate variation and explains why it returned.
- Path completion, communication-mode evidence, capability state, review need, and weekly consistency remain separate views.
- The learner chooses two, three, or five practice days per week, or no target. Missing a day erases nothing.
- One local calendar day counts after a completed authored lesson or strengthening review. Partial sittings, elapsed time, and repeated completions on the same day add no extra credit.
- An unfinished current week preserves the preceding rhythm until the week ends. Missing the completed week's target restarts the current rhythm while preserving the best rhythm and every learning record.
- Goal changes apply to the current and future weeks. Earlier weeks keep the target active at that time, and practice recorded before the first target cannot backfill a streak.

## Emotional transition

- **Before:** uncertainty, vulnerability, or guilt about consistency.
- **After:** calm competence, a truthful sense of progress, and one clear next action.
- **Avoid:** pressure to protect an app-owned number, generic praise, failure theater, or overstated mastery.

## Interaction details

- Correct micro-feedback is specific and brief.
- Incorrect feedback explains the sound relationship and keeps retry available.
- Lesson closure names the evidence state and distinguishes demonstration from retention.
- Due strengthening practice becomes the primary Home action; new material remains secondary until recovery is complete.
- Progress uses restrained surfaces, existing semantic colors, no decorative reward layer, and one weekly-rhythm control.
- A compact gold rhythm status opens a Monday-first calendar. Lesson closure shows quiet weekly progress and marks 1-, 4-, 12-, and 52-week milestones without sound, haptics, looping motion, or a blocking modal.
- After a lapse, the product says: "Your learning is still here. Start a new rhythm with one short lesson."

## Measurement contract

- **Primary learning metric:** first-try performance on a delayed, varied checkpoint.
- **Primary behavior metric:** meaningful practice days against the learner's chosen cadence.
- **Guardrails:** immediate repeat farming, post-recovery abandonment, recognition-only avoidance, weekly-target opt-out, accessibility failures, and any capability claim not reproducible from stored evidence.

This client-only slice validates the interaction and deterministic evidence policy. Authenticated cross-device attempt history, immutable content versions, richer review variants, and retained capability remain backend/content slices.
