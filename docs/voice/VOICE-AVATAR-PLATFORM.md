# Superseded Voice and Avatar Proposal

Status: **superseded for Core V1**

The former LiveKit, cascade-provider, animated-avatar, and generated-video proposal is not the
approved Voice implementation plan. Do not use it to add HeyGen, LiveAvatar, avatar rendering,
video generation, separate streaming speech providers, or platform video dependencies.

The current source of truth is [Direct OpenAI Realtime Voice](./VOICE-REALTIME.md). Any future
avatar or video proposal requires a separate product decision, privacy review, implementation
scope, and rollout plan.
